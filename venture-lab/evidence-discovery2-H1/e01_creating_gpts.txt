New GPT creation and publishing are not available on personal ChatGPT accounts, including Free, Go, Plus, and Pro.

Existing GPTs remain available to use and can still be edited if existing plan and permission requirements are met.

Users in Business, Enterprise, and Edu workspaces can create, edit, and publish GPTs when their workspace settings and permissions allow it.
